## Source hygiene

When contributing:

1. Prefer a primary text for direct quotations.
2. Do not copy editorial introductions from modern copyrighted editions.
3. Record edition differences when wording varies.
4. If a famous quote cannot be traced, treat it as unverified and exclude it from `SKILL.md`.
5. Remember that early Liang Qichao texts contain concepts and terminology shaped by their historical period. The skill should translate method, not reproduce outdated classifications as modern facts.
